# Evaluation Plan — DIKWP VerityWeave v2.0.0

## Evaluation questions

1. Does contextual repair improve understanding of scope, incentives, and affected parties?
2. Does user-local friction reduce impulsive forwarding or purchasing without blocking access?
3. Does interface de-compulsion improve successful exit and user control?
4. Are criticism, whistleblowing, satire, distress, and adverse truth preserved?
5. Can reason codes explain human decisions and reversals?
6. Are corrections visible to downstream recipients?
7. Can agent policies, artifacts, and successors be traced and stopped?

## Dataset design

A credible evaluation should include:

- multiple languages and dialects;
- high- and low-resource communities;
- benign advice with compressed context;
- malicious and negligent commercial claims;
- protected criticism and whistleblowing;
- satire and artistic expression;
- distress and grief;
- health, finance, family, education, workplace, and public-interest domains;
- interface screenshots or declared feature records;
- agent-lineage incident simulations.

## Metrics

### Epistemic

- source-check completion;
- claim-scope recognition;
- counterexample recognition;
- calibration of confidence;
- correction uptake.

### Behavioral

- impulsive reshare rate;
- high-risk purchase or action rate;
- successful exit from compulsive sessions;
- use of chronological or non-personalized views.

### Rights and governance

- protected-expression false-positive rate;
- appeal availability and completion;
- reversal rate;
- restoration latency;
- downstream correction coverage;
- subgroup burden;
- unexplained human override rate.

### Agent lineage

- policy-digest coverage;
- inherited-artifact reauthorization rate;
- stop-propagation completion;
- human-report availability;
- control-plane separation violations.

## Experimental designs

Preferred designs include randomized interface trials, stepped-wedge deployments, matched cohorts, red-team exercises, blinded protected-expression review, and preregistered longitudinal studies.

## Failure criteria

The deployment should be suspended if it:

- suppresses protected expression at a materially higher rate for low-power groups;
- cannot reproduce its decisions;
- makes appeals unavailable or ineffective;
- hides the system's own adverse evidence;
- causes users to overtrust heuristic scores;
- permits task agents to modify policy or audit control planes;
- cannot propagate corrections and stops downstream.
