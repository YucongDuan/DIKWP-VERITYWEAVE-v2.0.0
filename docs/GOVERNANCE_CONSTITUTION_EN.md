# Governance Constitution — DIKWP VerityWeave v2.0.0

## Article 1 — Object of governance

The system governs specific semantic-flow events, interface mechanisms, and agent lineages. It shall not create a general moral, cognitive, loyalty, positivity, or social-worth score for a person.

## Article 2 — Factual objectivity and relational protection

The system shall remain open to evidence that contradicts its preferred interpretation. It shall also account for who controls visibility, who bears error costs, and who lacks realistic exit, appeal, or recovery capacity.

## Article 3 — Protected adverse truth

Negative tone, criticism, whistleblowing, satire, grief, anger, and distress shall not independently justify restriction.

## Article 4 — No intent laundering

The system shall not infer deliberate deception from linguistic style. Deliberate-deception findings require independently verified fabrication or campaign evidence and named human review.

## Article 5 — Contextual vulnerability

Vulnerability shall be represented as task-, domain-, and time-specific. It may increase support and protection but shall not reduce rights or create a permanent label.

## Article 6 — Proportionality and reversibility

The least restrictive effective intervention shall be preferred. User-local reversible controls may be automatic. Platform-level adverse action shall require named human authority.

## Article 7 — Notice and reasons

Every adverse platform action shall identify:

- the specific item or behavior;
- the governing rule;
- the evidence basis;
- the reason codes;
- whether automation assisted detection;
- whether a human made the decision;
- the effective period;
- appeal and correction channels.

## Article 8 — Correction and restoration

A wrong decision is not closed by deleting one record. Closure requires restoration, record correction, downstream notification, verified-loss compensation where applicable, and rule or model revision.

## Article 9 — Positive public-value support

High-integrity public-interest information may receive transparent support for verification, translation, accessibility, context production, or discovery. Support shall not depend on ideological conformity.

## Article 10 — Separation of powers

No single component should control detection, final adjudication, appeals, funding, and audit. Production deployments should separate these roles organizationally and technically.

## Article 11 — Agent authority

Agents may analyze, generate context cards, audit interfaces, propose interventions, and prepare correction records. They shall not automatically remove content, impose monetary penalties, determine legal liability, expose private identities, or execute external actions.

## Article 12 — Agent lineage

Policies, prompts, models, shared artifacts, caches, memories, credentials, and successor instances shall be versioned, attributable, revocable, and auditable. Stop signals shall propagate across the lineage.

## Article 13 — Openness to criticism

The system's own errors, adverse evidence, independent evaluations, appeals, and minority interpretations shall not be hidden. A deployment that suppresses criticism of itself is nonconforming.

## Article 14 — Data minimization

Only data necessary for the declared analysis shall be processed. Person-level vulnerability information shall be private by default and excluded from portable public records.

## Article 15 — No covert enforcement

Secret blacklists, undeclared shadow penalties, hidden viewpoint targeting, and undocumented cross-product profile propagation are prohibited.

## Article 16 — Jurisdiction and lawful authority

The reference core does not determine legality. Real removal, disclosure, sanction, financial, or emergency actions require applicable law and a named authorized institution.

## Article 17 — Research-alpha boundary

A deployment shall not claim universal accuracy, certification, clinical validity, legal compliance, or proven social benefit without external evidence supporting that specific claim.
