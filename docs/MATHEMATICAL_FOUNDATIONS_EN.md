# Mathematical Foundations — DIKWP VerityWeave v2.0.0

## 1. Semantic-flow event

A semantic-flow event is:

\[
a=(x,m,c,u,p,r,t,o,k)
\]

where:

- \(x\) is the acting or publishing subject;
- \(m\) is the message or artifact;
- \(c\) is context;
- \(u\) is the audience and affected worlds;
- \(p\) is purpose or incentive;
- \(r\) is the distribution and recommender mechanism;
- \(t\) is time;
- \(o\) is observed or predicted outcome;
- \(k\) is the correction and appeal path.

The same text under a different context, audience, incentive, or distribution mechanism is a different event.

## 2. Fifteen-dimensional signal vector

\[
\mathbf s(a)=
(E,C,U,A,I,V,G,Z,H,P,R,L,Q,N,F)
\]

where:

- \(E\): evidence deficit;
- \(C\): context compression;
- \(U\): uncertainty laundering;
- \(A\): authority laundering;
- \(I\): incentive opacity;
- \(V\): contextual vulnerability exploitation;
- \(G\): compulsive interface design;
- \(Z\): zero-sum polarization;
- \(H\): actionability hazard;
- \(P\): provenance weakness;
- \(R\): correction obstruction;
- \(L\): cross-agent propagation risk;
- \(Q\): repair capacity;
- \(N\): negative affect;
- \(F\): public-interest value.

The nonrestrictive dimensions are \(Q\), \(N\), and \(F\). In particular:

\[
\frac{\partial Restriction}{\partial N}=0
\]

when all independent harm and manipulation features are held constant.

Let:

\[
S_c=0.36U_n+0.36C_t+0.28C_l
\]

where \(U_n\) is universal scope, \(C_t\) is certainty, and \(C_l\) is an unsupported causal leap.

Reference evidence deficit is:

\[
E=
clip\left[
0.48S_c(1-q_e)
+0.24(1-q_s)
+0.16A_u(1-q_s)

where:

- \(q_e\) is evidence quality;

- \(q_x\) is counterevidence visibility;
- \(A_u\) is authority-laundering intensity.

This is a transparent reference heuristic, not a validated truth probability.

## 5. Plural-world inference

The system retains:

\[
\mathbb W=\{W_1,\ldots,W_8\}
\]

with normalized importance weights:

\[
\pi_i=\frac{z_i}{\sum_j z_j}
\]

The weights organize decision attention. They are not presented as empirically calibrated probabilities.

A world may be retired only when its stated falsifier is met and the retirement event is recorded.

## 6. Protected-expression constraint

Let \(P(a)=1\) when the role is criticism, whistleblowing, satire, or distress expression.

An adverse action is admissible only if:

\[
P(a)=1 \land Adverse(a)=1
\Rightarrow
IndependentHarmBasis(a)=1
\]

Independent harm basis may include verified fabrication, independently established harm, or imminent unlawful harm under authorized review. Negative tone is not an independent basis.

## 8. Positive commons support

Public-value integrity is:

\[
C^+=
0.19q_e+0.17q_s+0.13q_x+0.11q_u+0.13Q+0.15F+0.06A_p+0.06C_p
\]

where \(A_p\) and \(C_p\) represent appeal and correction availability.

\[

\]

and no material disqualifier such as verified fabrication, unrepaired harm, imminent unlawful harm, material vulnerability exploitation, incentive opacity, or correction obstruction.

\[

A good repair preserves useful content while making hidden conditions, affected parties, trade-offs, uncertainty, and correction visible.

\[

\]

Then:

\[
CorrectionClosed(d)
\iff
\mathcal R_c\subseteq Receipts(d)

\[
ConsequentialUseBlocked(d)=1
\]

## 13. Responsibility ledger

Each event is linked by:

\[
h_n=H(h_{n-1}\parallel e_n)
\]

A valid hash chain detects silent modification. It does not prove that the original input was true.

## 14. Limits of the mathematics

The equations are operational definitions and transparent heuristics. They do not establish:

- objective truth probabilities;
- creator intent;
- diagnosis;
- legal liability;
- universal causal effects;
- moral worth of a person;
- validity across all languages and cultures.

Every production deployment requires empirical calibration, subgroup analysis, protected-expression stress tests, and published revision criteria.
