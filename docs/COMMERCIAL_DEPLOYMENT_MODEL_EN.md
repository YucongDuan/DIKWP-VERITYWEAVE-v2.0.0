# Deployable Open-Core Model — DIKWP VerityWeave v2.0.0

## Open core

The Apache-2.0 core includes:

- semantic-flow analysis;
- fifteen-dimensional signals;
- plural-world interpretation;
- context repair cards;
- positive-commons assessment;
- interface audit;
- agent-lineage audit;
- appeals and five-receipt correction;
- local API and MCP;
- schemas and reference protocol mappings;
- responsibility ledger;
- tests and bounded model checking.

## Sustainable paid services

Commercial value can come from deployment and assurance rather than hiding the core logic:

- multilingual and domain-specific calibration;
- enterprise identity, role, and workflow integration;
- signed policy bundles and transparency logs;
- platform and recommender connectors;
- independent protected-expression audits;
- appeals operations and restoration workflows;
- creator disclosure and correction tooling;
- child-safety and high-stakes-domain policy packs;
- agent-lineage observability;
- private deployment, monitoring, support, and service-level commitments;
- longitudinal effectiveness studies.

## Initial paid pilot

A practical twelve-week pilot can cover:

```text
one platform surface or community
one language and domain
one user-local intervention bundle
one creator repair workflow
one appeal and correction workflow
a protected-expression stress set
an agent-lineage audit
```

The pilot should not begin with automatic removal. Its first paid outcome is a defensible evidence package showing where manipulation enters the semantic flow, which interventions reduce control without suppressing adverse truth, and which processes need institutional change.

## Commercial falsification gates

Do not scale the deployment if:

- users and creators cannot understand the reasons;
- protected-expression false positives remain high;
- the platform will not provide a real appeal and restoration path;
- the buyer wants covert person-level scoring;
- the buyer refuses to disclose engagement incentives;
- the deployment cannot separate detection and final decision authority;
- the agent lineage cannot be audited;
- no measured behavior or outcome changes after context and friction interventions.
