# Source Register

Access date: 6 September 2026.

This register identifies external frameworks that informed the architecture. Inclusion does not imply endorsement, certification, or conformance.

1. **UNESCO — Guidelines for the Governance of Digital Platforms**  
   Use: human-rights-based platform governance, freedom of expression, access to information, risk assessment, transparency, independent oversight, user empowerment.  
   URL: https://www.unesco.org/en/internet-trust/guidelines

2. **European Commission — Digital Services Act transparency and systemic-risk materials**  
   Use: statements of reasons, risk assessment, recommender-system governance, notice and appeal concepts.  
   URL: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/digital-services-act-package

3. **European Commission — Guidelines on protection of minors under the Digital Services Act**  
   Use: safety and privacy by design, age-appropriate risk reduction, proportionality.  
   URL: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/protection-minors-guidelines

4. **European Commission — AI Act transparency guidelines**  
   Use: AI-generated and manipulated-content transparency reference.  
   URL: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/guidelines-ai-transparency-obligations

5. **C2PA — Technical Specification 2.4**  
   Use: content provenance and authenticity reference mapping.  
   URL: https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.4/index.html

6. **AT Protocol — Labels and Ozone moderation service**  
   Use: speech-versus-reach separation, stackable labels, label expiry, community-operated moderation.  
   URL: https://atproto.com/specs/label

7. **Model Context Protocol — Specification 2026-07-28**  
   Use: stateless tool and resource interface for bounded local agents.  
   URL: https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2026-07-28

8. **NIST — AI Risk Management Framework and Generative AI Profile**  
   Use: governance, mapping, measurement, management, provenance, pre-deployment testing, incident disclosure.  
   URL: https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework

9. **OWASP — Top 10 for Agentic Applications**  
   Use: tool misuse, identity and privilege abuse, memory poisoning, insecure communication, cascading failures, and rogue-agent risks.  
   URL: https://genai.owasp.org/resource/owasp-top-10-for-agentic-applications-for-2026/

10. **W3C PROV-O**  
    Use: provenance graph reference mapping.  
    URL: https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/

11. **United States Federal Trade Commission — Dark patterns and deceptive design**  
    Use: interface features that manipulate purchasing, privacy, cancellation, and user choice.  
    URL: https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/topics/truth-advertising/dark-patterns

## Source boundaries

- These sources do not validate VerityWeave's formulas or effectiveness.
- The protocol mappings are unsigned references.
- Legal obligations vary by jurisdiction and deployment.
- Current versions must be rechecked before production use.
