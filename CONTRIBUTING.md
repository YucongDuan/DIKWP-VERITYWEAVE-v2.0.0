# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they improve evidence quality, contextual repair, user control, appealability, multilingual fairness, or reproducibility.

A contribution that introduces a new risk signal must include:

- a behavior-level definition;
- a documented reason it is not a proxy for protected viewpoint or person-level status;
- at least one false-positive case;
- at least one protected adverse-truth case;
- a reversible intervention path;
- tests showing that negative affect alone cannot trigger restriction.

Platform enforcement connectors are outside the reference core. Propose them as separately permissioned adapters with current legal review and explicit human authority.
